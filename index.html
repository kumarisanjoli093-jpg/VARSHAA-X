MODEL LIMITATIONS & FAILURE MODES

When could this prediction fail?

  • Unusual environmental conditions: Sudden localized microbursts or rapid micro-climatic shifts.
  • Incomplete input data: Temporary outages or latency in satellite cloud-cover updates.
  • Sub-model disagreement: Variance between neural network and gradient-boosted tree sub-models.
  • Out-of-range parameters: Extreme weather events exceeding the prototype training distribution.
